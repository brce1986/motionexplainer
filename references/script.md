# G3: Script and beat sheet

## Script
- **Length:** ~2.2 spoken words per second (60s ≈ 120–135 words, with the logo hold at the end).
- **Structure:**
  1. **Hook (0–7s):** the everyday problem, concrete and specific.
  2. **Reveal:** "<Brand> is <one-line positioning>."
  3. **3–4 beats of how it works:** one verb each.
  4. **Proof:** verification, guarantees, numbers from the site.
  5. **The other side,** if two-sided.
  6. **Where/who.**
  7. **Lockup:** brand + tagline.
- Short sentences, plain words. Numbers are written the way they should be spoken in the VO text ("zero dollars"), and shown as numerals on screen.
- **Brand pronunciation:** if TTS may misread the name, respell it in the VO text only (e.g. "Prediépi"); the screen keeps the real spelling.
